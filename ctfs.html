Competition Format The competition consists of three rounds: Round 1 – Qualification (Online, Jeopardy) • Date: August 22, 2026 • Start Time: 09:00 (UTC+8) • Duration: 24 hours Round 2 – Semi-Final (Onsite, Jeopardy) • Date: September 5, 2026 • Location: Mongolia Round 3 – Final (Onsite, Attack & Defense) • Date: September 24, 2026 • Location: Mongolia Challenge Categories • Pwn • Web • Forensics • Cryptography • Reverse Engineering • Miscellaneous The challenges range from beginner to advanced difficulty, providing an engaging experience for both new and experienced CTF players.
