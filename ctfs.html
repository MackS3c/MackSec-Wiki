Haruulzangi CTF 2026 Qualifier

Início: 22 August 2026 - 01:00 UTC

Localzação: Online

Organizadores: haruulzangi-organizers

Descrição: Haruul Zangi CTF 2026 is organized by MNCERT/CC and the Haruulzangi CTF Team, representing the Mongolian CERT and cybersecurity community. We are proud to announce the 14th edition of Haruul Zangi CTF and welcome participants from around the world. The competition aims to promote practical cybersecurity skills, encourage knowledge sharing, and strengthen collaboration within the global cybersecurity community. Competition Format The competition consists of three rounds: Round 1 – Qualification (Online, Jeopardy) • Date: August 22, 2026 • Start Time: 09:00 (UTC+8) • Duration: 24 hours Round 2 – Semi-Final (Onsite, Jeopardy) • Date: September 5, 2026 • Location: Mongolia Round 3 – Final (Onsite, Attack & Defense) • Date: September 24, 2026 • Location: Mongolia Challenge Categories • Pwn • Web • Forensics • Cryptography • Reverse Engineering • Miscellaneous The challenges range from beginner to advanced difficulty, providing an engaging experience for both new and experienced CTF players. Team Information • Teams may consist of up to 3 players. • International participants are welcome. Qualification The following teams will advance to the onsite Semi-Final: The top 30 teams from the online Qualification Round, regardless of nationality. The highest-ranked Mongolian high school team, if not already qualified through the top 30 overall ranking. The highest-ranked Mongolian women’s team, if not already qualified through the top 30 overall ranking. Among the international teams that qualify for the Semi-Final, the two highest-ranked teams, based on the online Qualification Round results, will be invited directly to the onsite Final. If two international teams are invited to the Final, the top eight Mongolian teams from the Semi-Final will advance to the Final. The Final will consist of 10 teams in total and will be played in the Attack & Defense format. Support for International Finalists The organizers will provide the following support for the two invited international finalist teams: • Hotel accommodation during the onsite event. • A daily allowance to cover meals and local expenses. Invited international teams are responsible for: • International and domestic transportation. • Airfare and all other travel-related expenses. Visa invitation letters and supporting documents will be provided upon request. Previous Challenges Challenges from previous Haruul Zangi CTF competitions are publicly available in our GitHub organization: https://github.com/haruulzangi We look forward to welcoming teams from Mongolia and around the world to Haruul Zangi CTF 2026. Good luck, have fun, and see you in Mongolia!
